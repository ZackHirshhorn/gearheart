# GEARHEART Issue #1: "Built Here" (also styled "Coming Home")

> This is the written comic script (the screenplay). The matching AI image prompts live in [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md).

**Logline:** America never stopped inventing; it stopped making. A third-generation machinist named Dan Reyes-Holloway, who can bring dead factories back to life, helps the President's public push to bring manufacturing home, against a state-backed conglomerate trying to make the first plant fail.

**Theme line:** "We never stopped inventing. We stopped making."

---

## Shield Design Notes

A planetary gear set: a red/white outer ring gear with fine teeth and a blue striped side wall, six red planet gears each with a white star cut-out, and a thick red hollow central sun gear.

**Origin:** Dan's apprentice test piece, cut on the Ashford floor when he was 18; his father hung it over the tool crib. Not high-tech, just precision work, the kind of thing a working American plant makes every day. When Dan holds it, his gift spins the gears into position to block incoming projectiles. It is his emblem, not the driver of the plot.

**Signature move, the Torque Wave:** Dan kneels and drives the shield's rim into the ground; the planet gears spin up like a mill drive and the rotation sends a shock wave rolling outward, the ground bucks, gravel jumps, and anyone standing near is knocked down or thrown. It looks different on every surface (gravel, concrete dust, water spray, sparks on steel). Dan never fires anything at people; debris erupts upward and rains down.

## Prologue Splash (full page)

Ashford Falls, Ohio, at dawn. A shuttered steel and bearing plant by a frozen river. A rusted sign reads "ASHFORD STEEL - EST. 1911." A new banner going up over the gate: "COMING HOME: AMERICAN MANUFACTURING INITIATIVE."

**CAPTION:** "We designed the world's machines. Then we shipped the work of building them overseas."

**CAPTION:** "Now we're bringing it back."

---

## Page 1

**Panel 1:** The plant floor is packed with workers, cameras and flags. THE PRESIDENT speaks at a podium.

> **PRESIDENT:** "America never stopped inventing. The best engineers in the world are still right here. What we lost was the making. The factories. The jobs. That's what we're bringing home."

**Panel 2:** In the crowd, DAN REYES-HOLLOWAY, 34, a third-generation machinist and line setter in a denim jacket. Beside him, his old foreman WALT KOWALSKI, 61.

> **WALT:** "Believe him?"
>
> **DAN:** "I believe him. I just know how hard it is to move a factory."

**Panel 3:** The President points out at the crowd.

> **PRESIDENT:** "If we can't make our own steel, our own parts, our own medicine, then we depend on someone else for our survival. Not anymore."

---

## Page 2

**Panel 1:** The President tours the floor with ELAINE HOLLIS, CEO of Hollis Bearing Co. (her family company moved production overseas in 2004).

> **HOLLIS:** "We're bringing our whole line back. Sixty machines, two hundred jobs. But the people who set it up retired years ago. Nobody here knows how to stand it up."

**Panel 2:** The President stops at Dan's station. Dan is rebuilding a 1970s turret lathe by hand; it already runs smooth.

> **PRESIDENT:** "Who restored this?"
>
> **WALT:** "Him. Dan can set up a line faster than anybody I've seen in forty years."

**Panel 3:** The President looks from Dan to Hollis.

> **PRESIDENT:** "Then he's your man. Get that line home and get it running."

**Panel 4:** Hollis offers Dan her hand.

> **HOLLIS:** "Ninety days. Can you do it?"
>
> **DAN:** "Ship me the machines."

---

## Page 3: The Opposition

**Panel 1:** A boardroom overseas. Screens read MERIDIAN PACIFIC CONSORTIUM, a state-backed manufacturing giant that owns the plant Hollis is leaving.

**Panel 2:** DIRECTOR SHAO LIN-WEI watches the Ashford speech.

> **SHAO:** "If Hollis brings its line home and it works, fifty companies follow. Then a hundred. They won't need us."

**Panel 3:** Across from him, MARGUERITE VOSS, an American logistics fixer who sells to the highest bidder.

> **VOSS:** "Then the line never runs. Ships get delayed. Crates get damaged. Parts go missing. A factory that can't start is just a press release."

**Panel 4:** Shao slides over a file marked HOLLIS / ASHFORD.

> **SHAO:** "The President's program rests on the first plants succeeding. Make this one fail."

---

## Page 4: The Gift

**Panel 1:** Night. Dan and Walt restart the plant's old rolling mill for the first time in twenty years. A drive coupling cracks and a shaft whips loose toward Walt.

> **SFX:** "KRAK"

**Panel 2:** Dan grabs the live drive housing to wrench it away. A surge of current arcs through him.

> **SFX:** "KZZZRRRAAAK"

**Panel 3:** Later, in the hospital, Dan wakes up. A heart monitor beeps beside him. He turns his head toward it.

> **CAPTION (DAN):** "I could hear it. Not the beep. The machine. Its transformer was humming a little off."

**Panel 4:** Back on the plant floor, Dan touches a seized press, and it frees up and runs true.

> **CAPTION:** "Whatever happened that night, machines talk to me now. Where they're worn. Where they're out of true. Where they need to go."

---

## Page 5: What He Can Do

**Panel 1:** Dan walks an empty bay with chalk, marking the floor without measuring. Overlay: a ghosted image of a complete, perfectly laid-out production line.

> **CAPTION:** "I see a whole line before it's built."

**Panel 2:** He lays a hand on a worn grinder and it hums smoothly. A tolerance readout reads +/- 0.0001.

> **CAPTION:** "I make old machines run like new."

**Panel 3:** Dan stands with a dozen new hires around a machine, his hand over a trainee's.

> **CAPTION:** "And I can teach it. That matters most. A factory is people."

**Panel 4:** Walt hands Dan the shield, the old apprentice piece, from its place over the tool crib.

> **WALT:** "Your dad would want you to have this back. Looks like you'll need it."

---

## Page 6: The Convoy

**Panel 1:** The Port of Baltimore. Hollis's sixty machines arrive in crates, then load onto a freight train bound for Ohio.

**Panel 2:** Dan rides the train, with the shield strapped to his back and a hard hat on.

**Panel 3:** Night in the mountains of West Virginia. Trucks with no lights pull alongside the tracks. Mercenaries hired by Voss, led by KRAIT, raise their rifles.

> **KRAIT:** "Hit the flatcars. Wreck the machines. No line, no factory."

**Panel 4:** Dan stands on top of a freight car, lit by the moon.

> **DAN:** "Those are two hundred jobs. You don't touch them."

---

## Page 7: The Fight

**Panel 1:** The mercenaries open fire on the train.

**Panel 2 (Large):** Dan raises the shield. The gears spin, and the six star-gears orbit into place to block every round.

> **SFX:** "CHK-CHK-CHK-CHK-CHK-CHK"

**Panel 3:** Dan leaps from the freight car and lands hard on the gravel trackbed, between the train and the mercenaries' trucks.

> **KRAIT:** "He's alone. Rush him!"

**Panel 4 (Wide, splash-width):** Dan drops to one knee and drives the shield's rim into the gravel trackbed. The gears spin into a red, white and blue blur. A ring of gravel and dust erupts upward around him like a crown and rolls outward in a wave. The mercenaries are knocked off their feet. Stones hail down on them, and they throw their arms up over their faces. Rifles fly, and a truck rocks onto two wheels. In the center, inside a clear circle, Dan kneels untouched.

> **SFX:** "VRRRRRRMMMMM - THOOOOOM"
>
> **Small scattered SFX:** "tik tik tak tik"

**Panel 5 (Small):** Dan, still kneeling, looks up while the gears spin down.

> **CAPTION (DAN):** "Every machine has a drive. This one's mine."

**Panel 6:** Krait, dazed in the dirt, slams his hand down on a detonator.

> **SFX:** "KRA-THOOM"

---

## Page 8: Derailed

**Panel 1:** The train jumps the track. Crates spill down the embankment.

**Panel 2:** Morning. Dan stands among the wreckage. Machines lie broken, scattered across the hillside.

**Panel 3:** Hollis arrives, devastated.

> **HOLLIS:** "That's it. The insurance alone will take months. The program's first plant, and it's dead on a hillside."

**Panel 4:** Dan kneels and puts his hand on a cracked spindle housing.

> **DAN:** "No. They're hurt, not dead. I can hear every one of them."

---

## Page 9: The Build

**Panel 1:** Montage. Ashford workers drive down with flatbeds and cranes to haul the machines home.

**Panel 2:** Day after day, Dan walks the bay, matching parts and pointing. Workers rebuild, reweld, and realign.

> **CAPTION (DAN):** "Some parts we machined ourselves. Some came from shops in Michigan, Indiana, and Pennsylvania that were still hanging on."

**Panel 3:** Walt teaches a group of high-school kids on a lathe.

> **WALT:** "Your granddads did this. Now it's your turn."

**Panel 4:** A calendar on the wall: DAY 87. The line is complete and gleaming.

---

## Page 10: Start-Up Day

**Panel 1:** The ribbon-cutting. Hollis, the workers, and cameras gather. Krait, disguised as a contractor, slips away from the main drive.

**Panel 2:** Hollis reaches for the start button. Dan freezes.

> **CAPTION (DAN):** "Something was wrong. The main drive was screaming."

**Panel 3:** Dan leaps. The shield catches a pressure fitting as it blows, and its spinning gears contain the burst.

> **SFX:** "KSSHHHH-CLANG"

**Panel 4:** Dan chases Krait through the plant. Workers block the exits with forklifts, and Krait is cornered.

---

## Page 11

**Panel 1:** Federal agents arrest Krait. His phone shows a message thread with Voss.

**Panel 2:** Voss is arrested at a private airfield.

> **VOSS:** "It's one plant. You can't move a whole economy."

**Panel 3:** Dan, calm:

> **DAN:** "We're not moving it. We're bringing it home, one line at a time."

**Panel 4:** Back at Ashford, Hollis presses the button. The line roars to life.

> **SFX:** "VRRRRRRRMMMMM"

---

## Page 12

**Panel 1:** The first bearing rolls off the line. Walt picks it up and holds it to the light.

> **WALT:** "Made in Ashford. Thought I'd never say that again."

**Panel 2:** The President returns for the opening, in public, on camera.

> **PRESIDENT:** "They said it couldn't be done. Two hundred jobs, ninety days. This is the first of many. Forty more companies have signed on to bring their lines home this year."

**Panel 3:** Afterward, he speaks with Dan privately.

> **PRESIDENT:** "Every factory that comes back makes us stronger, and makes us harder to push around. If we don't do this, we'll depend on our rivals for everything. We can't let that happen."

**Panel 4:** Dan nods.

> **DAN:** "Then send me the next one."

**Panel 1:** Dan stands on the plant roof at sunrise, with the shield on his back. Below, trucks marked MADE IN USA roll out the gate, workers flood in, and smoke rises from the stacks.

> **CAPTION:** "We never lost the ideas. We lost the hands."
>
> **CAPTION:** "I'm here to bring them back."
