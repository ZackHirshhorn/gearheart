# GEARHEART #1: Coming Home (Prologue + Pages 1-3 + Page 7)

An interactive comic prototype. All art is hand-authored SVG generated by plain JavaScript. No build step, no network, no dependencies.

## The story so far

Ashford Falls, Ohio. The steel plant is shuttered, but a new national program is bringing American manufacturing home. At a rally on the old plant floor, the President tells the country what was lost was never the inventing, only the making. Dan Reyes-Holloway, a third-generation machinist and line setter, is picked by Elaine Hollis (CEO of Hollis Bearing Co.) to stand up her relocated production line in ninety days. Overseas, Meridian Pacific Consortium and a logistics fixer named Marguerite Voss decide the line must never run.

Pages 4-6 are not drawn yet (Dan's current surge saves Walt, the machines talk to him, Walt gives him the shield from over the tool crib, Hollis's machines ship by freight from Baltimore to Ohio). The viewer shows a "coming" note, and the issue jumps ahead to **Page 7**: the Torque Wave fight on a night freight train in the West Virginia mountains.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html`. It runs from `file://` in any modern browser.

## Controls

- **Prev / Next buttons** or **Left / Right arrow keys**: turn the page (Prologue, Page 1, Page 2, Page 3, Page 7). Home / End jump to the first / last page.
- **Shield Lab** button, or click the Torque Wave panel on Page 7: opens the Shield demo.
  - **State** button: toggles the Shield between DORMANT and ACTIVE (gears spin, planet gears orbit, motion blur lines, sparks).
  - **Hover** over the Shield to aim: the nearest planet gear slides into the line of fire.
  - **Click** to send a test projectile at the Shield; the planet gear blocks it with a spark burst.
  - **Torque Wave** button: the Shield is planted rim-first in gravel and spins up. A ring of gravel and dust erupts upward around it, rolls outward as a wave and rains back down, leaving a clear calm circle around the Shield. Nothing is fired at anyone. Press again to stop or reset.
  - **Auto-fire** button sends test projectiles at random angles. **Esc** closes the lab.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected: spinning is off, the lab's movements snap instead of animating, and the Torque Wave shows a single still frame of the eruption.

## The Shield

A planetary gear set: a red and white outer ring gear with fine teeth and a blue striped side wall, six red planet gears each with a white star cut-out, and a thick red hollow sun gear. Dan cut it as an apprentice test piece on the Ashford floor at 18, and his father hung it over the tool crib. Signature move, the **Torque Wave**: Dan kneels and drives the shield's rim into the ground, the planet gears spin up like a mill drive, and the rotation sends a shock wave rolling outward.

## Files

- `index.html`: page shell, navigation, Shield Lab dialog
- `style.css`: layout, caption boxes, speech bubbles, SFX, Shield animation
- `js/art.js`: all SVG art (panels and the Shield)
- `js/app.js`: page/panel layout data, script text, navigation, Shield Lab and Torque Wave logic
- `check.js`: sanity check (`node check.js`): renders every panel listed in `js/app.js`, confirms the SVG is well-formed, and checks the Shield has 6 planet gears and 6 stars
- `PROMPTS.md`: AI image prompt pack
